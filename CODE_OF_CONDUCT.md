# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a good environment:

- being kind and patient, especially with newcomers,
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully,
- focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it,
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults, or derogatory comments,
- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances,
- personal or political attacks,
- publishing others' private information without permission,
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they deem inappropriate.

This applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, Shams Ali Shaikh, through GitHub's "Report content" feature or by contacting them via the repository owner's GitHub profile. All reports are reviewed and handled with discretion and respect for the reporter's privacy.

Consequences, in increasing order: a private warning, a public warning, a temporary ban, a permanent ban.
